# Tedix

**A platform for agents, apps, and work.**

Tedix is a shared home for people and AI agents to work together. Keep your
team's tasks, project knowledge, apps, decisions, and results in one place.

Use the AI assistant you already work with, such as Claude Code or Codex,
and give it the information and tools it needs. Your team can see who is
doing what, review the results, and decide what happens next.

- [Tedix](https://github.com/tedix-hq/tedix): the platform and its source.
- [Tedix plugins](https://github.com/tedix-hq/tedix-plugins): connect your assistant.
- [Tedix Connect](https://docs.tedix.dev/learning-paths/first-plugin): connect through MCP.
- [Documentation](https://docs.tedix.dev): setup, examples, and guides.
- [Website](https://tedix.dev) · [Questions and ideas](https://github.com/tedix-hq/tedix/discussions)

Tedix Cloud is available by invitation. You can also try the local demo
without an account. See [release status](https://docs.tedix.dev/release-status).
